07 FAQs

Questions, answered

Good decisions
start with clarity.

Here are the basics. For a personal answer, reach us on WhatsApp or email the Secretary.

Who can join Rising Stars?

People in Kampala and across Uganda who want to save consistently, learn about money, and participate in shared investment can register their interest.

What is the difference between membership and shares?

Membership is the one-time entry fee that makes you part of the union. Shares are your ongoing ownership contributions, made weekly or monthly according to the group rules.

How are investments and returns handled?

Members receive updates on projects and financial performance. The Treasurer maintains records and the leadership team communicates approved decisions transparently.

Can I pay by Mobile Money or card?

Yes. The payment area supports MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and Visa or Mastercard. Official receiver details will be confirmed before payment.
